# A Correction to Traditional Tarot

The Sunlight Tarot was born from a need to address a fundamental problem in modern tarot: the prevalence of "black decks" and their potential for psychological harm. While acknowledging the historical and symbolic richness of decks like the Aleister Crowley Thoth Tarot, this project posits that their reliance on grotesque, demonic, or fear-inducing imagery can be counterproductive, especially for individuals seeking healing or navigating vulnerable psychological states.

Traditional tarot, particularly in the 20th century, often embraced a dark aesthetic, believing it necessary to represent the "shadow" aspects of the psyche. However, this can lead to a form of symbolic violence. When a person in crisis pulls a card like "Ruin" or a "Tower" depicted with fire and falling bodies, the image can reinforce fear and despair rather than providing a path to understanding or integration.

The Sunlight Tarot is a deliberate course-correction. It is an attempt to create a "white deck"—a deck that is bright, shining, and life-affirming in its core symbolism, following the path laid out by visionaries like Eileen Connolly. This does not mean ignoring life's difficulties. Instead, it means reframing them.

*   **Heartbreak** is not a heart pierced by swords, but an opportunity for the heart to open and bloom.
*   **Failure** is not a ruin, but fertile ground for new growth.
*   **Limitation** is not an external devil, but a self-imposed chain that we ourselves hold the key to.

By transforming the visual language of the tarot, we transform its psychological impact. We move from a system that can inadvertently reinforce trauma to one that actively supports healing, resilience, and the awakening of consciousness. The goal is to build a responsible tarot—a tool that is safe, gentle, and empowering for everyone, regardless of their state of mind.
