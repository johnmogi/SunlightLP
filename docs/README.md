# Sunlight Tarot - Build Guide

Welcome to the Sunlight Tarot project! This is an open-source tarot deck rebuilt around 5 elements with a focus on healing and inward journeying.

## What is Sunlight Tarot?

The Sunlight Tarot is a reimagined tarot system that:
- **Rebuilds the deck around 5 elements** (Aether, Air, Fire, Water, Earth)
- **Transforms the Major Arcana** into the Aether Element - representing consciousness itself
- **Creates healing imagery** - a "white deck" focused on empowerment rather than fear
- **Integrates the Enneagram system** to unlock deeper wisdom

## The Five Elements

| Element | Suit | Color | Mythology | Represents |
|---------|------|-------|-----------|------------|
| **Aether** | Major Arcana | Gold/White | Universal | Consciousness, Spirit, WHO you are |
| **Air** | Roses | Blue | Asian, Egyptian, Persian | Mind, Thought, Intellect |
| **Fire** | Cards | Green | Indigenous American | Will, Creation, Manifestation |
| **Water** | Hearts | Pink | Viking, Atlantean, Celtic | Emotion, Feeling, Heart |
| **Earth** | Coins | Brown | African, Aboriginal | Material, Body, Groundedness |

## Getting Started

1. Read the [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) guide to understand how to create cards
2. Explore the existing cards in `images/updatedCards/`
3. Join the community and vote on artwork at the website
4. Submit your artwork following the style guidelines
