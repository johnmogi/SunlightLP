# Contributing to Sunlight Tarot

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the Sunlight Tarot deck! This guide will help you create cards that fit our vision.

## Card Design Philosophy

### The Healing Approach
Unlike traditional "dark" tarot decks that can instill fear, Sunlight Tarot uses:
- **Empowering imagery** - transformation instead of destruction
- **Light-filled compositions** - even in shadow themes
- **Personal growth focus** - cards as mirrors for self-understanding

### Style Guidelines

1. **Art Styles We Use:**
   - Watercolor with soft edges
   - Pencil sketches with organic lines
   - Mixed media with botanical elements
   - Sepia/vintage diary aesthetic

2. **Color Palettes by Element:**
   - **Aether (Major):** Gold, white, celestial tones
   - **Roses (Air):** Blues, silvers, sky tones
   - **Cards (Fire):** Greens, earth tones, warm browns
   - **Hearts (Water):** Pinks, aquas, pearl tones
   - **Coins (Earth):** Browns, ochres, terra cotta

3. **Character Design:**
   - Each element has associated mythology (see README)
   - Robe colors match element
   - Diverse representation across cultures

## Creating a New Card

### Step 1: Choose Your Card
- Review existing cards in the deck
- Identify gaps or cards needing variants
- Consider the card's traditional meaning

### Step 2: Transform the Meaning
Transform traditional meanings to healing focus:

| Traditional | Sunlight Approach |
|-------------|-------------------|
| The Tower (destruction) | The Lighthouse (guidance through change) |
| Death | Transformation/Renewal |
| The Devil | Shadow Integration |

### Step 3: Design Elements

Every card should include:
- **Central figure or symbol**
- **Element indicators** (colors, symbols)
- **Meaningful background**
- **Clear visual narrative**

### Step 4: File Format

- **Format:** PNG (transparent or with background)
- **Size:** Minimum 1024x1536px (2:3 ratio)
- **Naming:** `[card_name]_[description]_[unique_id].png`

## Submitting Your Work

1. **Fork the repository**
2. **Add your card** to the appropriate element folder
3. **Update the card data** in `script.js` if needed
4. **Submit a pull request** with:
   - Card name and meaning
   - Visual description
   - Your artist credit

## The Sun Rosetta System

The Sun card is the origin - place the four Aces around it to see how elements emerge from consciousness:

```
         [Ace of Roses]
              (Air)
                |
[Ace of Cards]--[SUN]--[Ace of Coins]
   (Fire)       |        (Earth)
                |
         [Ace of Hearts]
             (Water)
```
